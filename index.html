Research

Cogan Lab

Developed end-to-end MNE-Python pipelines for intracranial sEEG analysis, from Audacity response annotation and high-gamma extraction to permutation-based electrode visualization for speech perception decoding. Analyzed 26 patient datasets to identify phoneme-dependent uniqueness points and characterize inter-subject variability in high-gamma neural representations.

Chory Lab

Engineered automated T7 phage-directed evolution workflows on Hamilton liquid handlers for 72-hour autonomous operation. Reduced PCR and Qubit reaction cost by over 50% on the Opentrons OT-2, contributed PyLabRobot visualizer modules, and documented automation protocols for future use.

bioRxiv Publication

Barrett Lab

Conducted Cell Painting Assay on induced iPSC-derived neurons and progenitor cells to study Down Syndrome morphology. Built an XGBoost pipeline to rank discriminative imaging features, achieving 83% accuracy in classifying Down Syndrome versus control cells.

HSURV Abstract Book pg. 44

Musah Lab

Analyzed RNA-seq data from iPSC-derived podocytes under injury conditions to identify potential therapeutic targets for chronic kidney disease. Gained hands-on experience with stem cell culture, injury treatment, and organ-on-chip technology; second-author manuscript under review at Stem Cell Reports.

Stem Cell Reports Publication

Navid Lab

Designed a primal-dual constraint learning framework for protein pathogenicity and fitness score prediction on ProteinNPT data. Fine-tuned ESM2 8M with LoRA on about 700k variants, improved Spearman correlation by 2% over baselines, and built a scalable 5-fold PyTorch validation pipeline.
